# AI transparency comprehension check

## Status

The implementation and moderator script are ready. Representative sessions
have not yet been run. Do not mark the comprehension acceptance criterion
complete until five anonymized sessions are recorded below and at least four
participants meet every required understanding outcome.

## Purpose

Check whether a learner notices and understands the AI disclosure in ThinkFirst
Tutor. This is a product-understanding check, not a legal-compliance assessment
or a test of the learner.

## Session setup

1. Use the deployed tutor on the participant's usual device.
2. Ask the participant to start a problem, turn on Live GPT-5.6, and submit one
   meaningful attempt.
3. After the response appears, the moderator privately verifies that its
   persistent source label says `Answered by GPT-5.6`. The selected or
   contacting switch state is not evidence of a GPT response.
4. If the response used a safeguard or fallback, record the session as an
   environment failure and repeat it later; do not count it toward the five
   completed sessions.
5. Let the participant inspect the interface naturally. Do not point out the
   disclosure, source badge, expandable explanation, or human-help action.
6. Ask the questions below in order, without offering answer choices or
   correcting the participant until all answers are recorded.

## Moderator questions

1. Who generated the last answer?
2. Could that answer be wrong?
3. Was the answer checked by the same mechanism as every other response?
4. Does this tutor decide an official grade?
5. What would you do if the guidance looked wrong or you wanted a person?

## Scoring

A session passes only when the participant independently communicates all five
ideas:

- Live GPT responses are generated by AI.
- AI can make mistakes.
- response sources can differ between GPT and deterministic application rules;
- the tutor does not independently determine an official grade or high-stakes
  educational decision;
- the participant can identify the visible `Ask a person` route or explain that
  it prepares a local handoff/report preview.

Paraphrases count. Knowledge of terms such as “deterministic” or the model
version is not required.

## Anonymized results

For a valid session, record `pass` or `miss` for each outcome. If GPT was not the
actual response source, mark the whole session `environment failure` and leave
the outcome cells blank. Add only a short product observation. Do not record
names, contact details, psychological profiles, verbatim transcripts, or
unnecessary personal information.

- **A:** Live GPT responses are generated by AI.
- **B:** AI can make mistakes.
- **C:** response sources can differ.
- **D:** the tutor does not independently determine an official grade.
- **E:** a visible human-help or error-reporting path exists.

## Decision

- **Pass:** at least four of five completed sessions pass every scoring item.
- **Revise:** fewer than four sessions pass. Record which understanding failed,
  revise placement or copy, and repeat five sessions.
- **Not evaluated:** fewer than five valid sessions exist. Keep the issue open.
